The Meeting Survival Kit

What to say in the first 10 seconds when your mind goes blank — or you get asked an unexpected question.

What to avoid

Sitting in dead silence or saying “Uhhh… wait.”

What to say

“That’s an interesting angle. Give me just a second to gather my thoughts on this.”

Why it works

You buy time while sounding calm, senior and thoughtful — not caught off guard.

These phrases will save your meeting tomorrow. But memorizing five sentences won't change your career. Here is what is actually happening in your brain when you freeze.

Part 2

Why You Actually Freeze

Why experienced professionals with 15+ years of career experience still feel insecure on international calls.

01

The 100mph Translation Trap

In your native language your thoughts are fast and complex. When you switch to English, you try to translate every thought word-for-word. Your brain overloads, runs out of memory — and your voice locks up.

02

The Perfection Fever

You're an expert at work. You hate looking silly or unprepared. So your brain says: “If you can't say it perfectly, don't say it at all.” You stay silent, nod, and let someone else take the credit for the deal because they speak faster.

03

The Traditional Course Illusion

You think the fix is more grammar rules or YouTube videos. The truth: the more rules you cram in, the more you overthink before opening your mouth. And the more you overthink, the more you freeze.
